Declared before the additional recovery experiments, following the independent `gaps_found` review. ## R1: matched comparator and demographic cultural accumulation

Use new extension modules only. Implement an action-conditional, observation-limited learner with explicit matched query accuracy and realized opportunity/computation costs. A bounded cost-aware planner is acceptable; do not call a greedy/short-horizon heuristic general optimal RL. Preserve private hardware accuracy .8 and all common demographic/resource parameters. Compare against ordinary social, existing feature trust, and the previously frozen candidate vectors in sentinel regimes 1, 5, 9, with 24 fresh paired seeds starting 81001. Freeze source/config before running; any extension is in outcome-blind batches of eight, maximum 96, retaining unresolved precision and every failed/extinct/censored run. Separate MCSE of paired abundance/energy outcomes from event proportions.

Build a minimal genuinely demographic accumulation robustness model, explicitly labeled as an additional model rather than silently replacing primary V7. Cultural innovations must be obtained by paid physical acquisition and reusable across individuals; no experts or acquired state may be inherited at birth. Compare transmission+innovation, no transmission, equal total-work independent solvers, prerequisite shuffle, carrier removal with storage off, and separately costed storage on. Use 24 fresh paired seeds starting 82001, fixed horizons/caps declared in the runner registration before outcomes. Record individual/population resources, paid work, positive functional acquisitions, biological and cultural carriers, post-loss knowledge, and demographic outcomes. A cognitive-only probe or infeasible pooled super-learner cannot satisfy this test. Carrier removal is actual removal; contemporaneous no-innovation checks distinguish lost memory from later reinvention. Negative/null results are acceptable.

## R2: ecological causal/provenance and sequential adult diagnostics

Use a separate extension over immutable corrected interfaces. Record labels, opportunities, chosen actions, conflict, source roots, work and material costs, and conditional versus unconditional counts. Use at least 24 fresh paired seeds starting 83001, with any batch extension fixed before examining effect signs.

Run 24 fresh paired adult training histories starting 84001 and the actual declared 20-step intervention horizon in both evidence directions. Audience removal, free audit, and matched-strength contrary/supporting evidence must update sequential acquired states. Keep belief revision, learned action persistence, source trust, and outcomes separate, with direct adult inherited override zero. Do not create an adult rigidity parameter proportional to d. Export every step and endpoint, not just an intervention duration field. Preserve material ledgers. Export purchase/no-teacher/conflict/revision opportunities and numerators. Apply the original precision rules where statistically appropriate; report unresolved Wilson precision at the declared cap and do not count correlated repeated observations as independent binomial trials.

## Common evidence and limits

Each extension must persist its own scientific definitions, Given/When/Then checks, registration with source/config hashes and fresh seeds, executable runner, meaningful tests, raw output, measured machine-readable verification, and concise results. Source freeze precedes scientific outcomes. Keep the numerical model readable and do not add positive deference rewards or tune to force an optimum. Distinguish coupled-model sensitivity findings from the corrected primary runs. If a requested test is infeasible within a bounded implementation, record the exact missing mechanism; do not manufacture a passing status.
