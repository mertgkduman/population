Conditional invasion: mutant eigenvalue at resident N*. Survival to maturation=m/(m+μF); mean stage-exit time=1/(m+μF).

τ is the mean wait without death, not a fixed birthday or the observed age of surviving adults. Adult advice quality is externally fixed within each sweep and accessible without capacity limits. M1 prescribes adult fecundity, not knowledge accumulation. Invasion assumes perfect trait inheritance, mutant rarity, fixed resident equilibrium, identical advice distribution, and shared resource limitation. Positive invasion shows initial growth only. Tiny positive deterministic populations are not literal extinction. Treating deference as an independently variable trait is a modeling assumption.
