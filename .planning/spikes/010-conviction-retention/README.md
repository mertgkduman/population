## Results
**PARTIAL.** Frozen convictions can outperform continuous bounded-score updating under stable noisy evidence; auditing often does better. Action and storage ablations separate current override from cultural acquisition. Exact Bayesian control prevents a claim of universal benefit from refusing valid cost-free evidence. Confidence-filtered tutors help some stable repertoires and preserve obsolete beliefs after change.

See [results](../../../v5/results.md) and [verification](../../../v5/verification.md). Scientific claims remain conditional on specified information, memory, query-cost and inheritance assumptions. No conclusion about a distinct historical childhood adaptation follows from software verification.
