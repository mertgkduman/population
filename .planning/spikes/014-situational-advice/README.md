## Results

**VALIDATED technically.** Main data:9,504 population and9,504 shared-resident mutant trajectories. Independent dense scans add6,336 population trajectories at33 d values;1,320 ablation trajectories isolate transmission versus action. No joint, fixed-tau or dense-held-out interior deference maximum was observed. Poor adult context information favors d=0; better adult knowledge can favor d=1 in stable settings or at short maturation. Therefore d=1 is not a situation-invariant optimum, and adding noise alone does not imply a middle optimum.

At tau=3, adult local precision.85 favors d=1 in stable/slow environments and d=0 in faster environments. At precision.70, clean explicit factual teaching favors d=1 in the stable ablation while interpreted advice and action-only substitution favor d=0. The transmission mechanism is materially assumption-sensitive. No distinct historical dogmatism adaptation is established.
