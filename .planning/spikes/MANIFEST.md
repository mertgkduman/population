# Spike Manifest

## Ideas

### deference-demography
Test the specified hypothetical two-state demographic mechanism across follower deference and maturation timescale. The spike yields analytic and numerical maps, a browser dashboard, and measured verification; it makes no historical adaptation claim.

**Requirements:**
- Hold private judgment, adult advice, resource constants, and initial composition fixed within a trait sweep.
- Keep exactly two demographic ODE states with irreversible maturation.
- Separate copying from weighted judgment and M0 from M1.
- Preserve negative, null, and boundary results.
- Provide a reproducible offline dashboard and source-backed data.

### developmental-cultural-evolution
Investigate inherited juvenile deference and maturation using endogenous cultural memory, explicit private/public distinctions, shared-culture invasion, and reliable-learning comparisons. V4 is original exploratory modeling, with novelty assessed against primary literature.

**Requirements:**
- Exactly two demographic stages per genotype; additional cognitive states permitted.
- Fixed private signal quality across inherited d and tau.
- Adult testimony generated from retained juvenile/adult beliefs; no direct tau reward or dogmatism genotype.
- Shared resident culture/resources for rare mutants; distinguish selection from isolated growth.
- Five focused quick spikes, preregistered hypotheses, adversarial tests, offline dashboard, full reproducibility.
- V6: juvenile private encoding more plastic than adult encoding, inherited ledgers retained at maturation, common stochastic environmental paths, and explicit equal-plasticity control.
- V6 publication order: V5, Further work, V6. The user subsequently authorized pushing and adding noisier situational advice; preserve the baseline as a distinct V6 comparison.
- In the V6 advice extension, adult current-situation signals are less accurate than juvenile signals, inherited judgment ability remains fixed, and an interior optimum must not be forced.
- V7: treat `d=0` as ordinary social learning and keep the asocial access-off comparator distinct.
- V7: preserve the frozen core and archived replay; reporting may recompute raw aggregates but must not alter model or configuration bytes.
- V7: use fixed age threshold `tau=5`, with genetic override disabled for adults at age 5 and above; do not describe this as an optimized maturation mean.
- V7: preserve negative, null, partial, and not-modeled outcomes; finite descendants must not be promoted to invasion, fixation, ESS, or stable polymorphism.
- V7: summarize rates across independent seeds; Wilson/binomial precision is unresolved unless event numerator and denominator counts are exported.
- V7 post-execution correction: preserve the frozen hidden-local-oracle source/raw trail as invalidated; use a separately registered three-method overlay and fresh seeds for corrected ecology/lineage inference.

### versioned-research-publication
Prepare V1–V5 as a static GitHub Pages research archive with Further work proposals.

**Requirements:**
- Keep scientific version files/data/specifications separate and unchanged.
- Original publication: six top tabs (V1–V5 and Further work). V6 adds a seventh after Further work; retain model/results/changes/shortcomings for every version.
- Put an exact plot-data ZIP and reference-data link beneath every interactive figure.
- Explain versions and all equations in main README.
- Respect file/site limits and preserve original history through a clean publishable export.
- Merge into local main; do not deploy publicly during preparation.
