# Further work: proposed investigations

These are proposals. They have not been simulated or verified as new models. Existing measurements remain in V1–V5.

## 1. Correlated teachers and cultural genealogy

**Question:** Does deference help when several confident adults repeat one ancestral observation?

Track the identity and origin of observations. Compare independent testimony against cloned sources with matched marginal accuracy; measure whether learners discount redundancy. A moment-based benchmark can control Cov(e_i,e_j)=rho sigma_i sigma_j, but source ancestry needs an explicit transmission mechanism.

**Falsifiable test:** an apparent multi-teacher gain should disappear when observations are informational duplicates and a learner knows that dependence.

## 2. Costly search for formed convictions

V5's confidence filter could search an entire cohort without cost. Match accepted-testimony counts before adding finite search, teacher capacity and search mortality cost c_search times actual search rate.

**Question:** Do confidence-qualified tutors still improve outcomes when locating them consumes time or resources? Include confidently wrong tutors and environmental change. Distinguish availability, accuracy and search expenditure.

## 3. Calibrated, resource-limited trust and stopping

Compare fixed deference with learned reliability, approximate Bayesian change detection and cost-sensitive sampling at equal storage and computation budgets. Keep childhood private knowledge for every comparator.

The stable independent-evidence control is the existing V5 Bayesian benchmark: optimal cost-free use of valid extra information cannot lower expected decision accuracy. A distinct deference mechanism must therefore be identified under declared costs, dependence or uncertainty, rather than by handicapping the updating comparator.

## 4. Ecological tasks with causal structure

Replace independent binary facts with resource rules, danger cues and causal dependencies. Hold cognitive architecture fixed while changing evidence access. Separate uncertainty about facts from coordination conventions and cooperation payoffs.

**Question:** Does copying a useful rule generalize, or does it propagate a coherent but wrong rule after a change?

## 5. Developmental exposure and evolving query policies

Test age-structured or delay-based development against exponential maturation. Let query choices develop through experience or evolve as explicit policies; do not label an assigned frozen mode emergent adult dogmatism. Preserve rare-mutant access to the resident cultural environment.

## 6. Empirical identification

Manipulate private cue quality, source confidence, source accuracy and search cost separately. Record private belief, public choice, confidence, later correction and knowledge transfer. Compare real rule changes with transient misinformation, providing extra feedback in conditions where the causes are distinguishable.

Laboratory copying choices alone cannot establish genetic independence, reproductive fitness or the historical evolution of human childhood. Those connections need additional evidence.
