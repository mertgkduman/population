Equations and limits
σA(t)=clamp[σA(0) exp(−g t), σfloor, σceiling] g>0 improves adult precision; g<0 degrades it. biasA and ρ stay fixed. Copy: L(d,t)=(1−d)σI²+d[σA(t)²+biasA²] Weighted judgment: L(d,t)=(1−d)²σI²+d²[σA(t)²+biasA²]+2ρd(1−d)σIσA(t) μF(d,t)=μF,base+kerror L(d,t) m=1/τ; S(N)=S₀/(1+N/Kₙ); β=βmax(τ)S/(Kₛ+S) F′=βA−[m+μF(d,t)]F; A′=mF−μA A; N′=B−D B=βA; D=μF F+μA A; M=mF M0: βmax=βconst. M1: βmax=βmin+βgain τ/(τ+τlearning). rT=log[N(T)/N(0)]/T. r_inst(t): frozen-time low-density eigenvalue.

The quality drift is externally prescribed and shared by all adults; it does not track individual adult experience, knowledge transmission, or teaching workload. There are still only two demographic ODE states. With g≠0, the system is nonautonomous, so a single stationary r₀, N*, or simple resident invasion eigenvalue is not claimed. A positive r_inst(t) is a local frozen-time diagnostic, not a fitness proof over the whole trajectory. τ remains an exponential maturation waiting time. No direct reward for d is added. Deterministic tiny positive populations are not literal extinction.
