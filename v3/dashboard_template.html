Q(t) and d(t) are prescribed functions of population calendar time, not new ODE states. All adults share the same advice distribution; this model does not resolve individual adult learning or juvenile-age-dependent deference. The choice to let Q affect advice variance through 1/(1+Q) is hypothetical. Positive instantaneous r does not establish persistence or invasion over the whole nonautonomous trajectory. No direct reward for d or Q is included. Deterministic near-zero populations are not literal extinctions. A technical verification verdict does not validate an evolutionary adaptation.
