Interpretation and hard limits
The map and trajectories are stored simulation outputs. Changing a control selects a precomputed context or cell; it does not solve equations in the browser. Finite-horizon growth summarizes population change over the full stored run. Last-100-time-unit log growth uses only the tail window and is not an asymptotic, stationary, Floquet, or Lyapunov result. Invasion is the rare mutant's conditional growth in a frozen resident culture and resource environment and is available only where that calculation was valid. None is fixation probability.
The fixed mechanism estimates deference plus internalization unless the public-only model is selected. The oracle reads contemporaneous adult accuracy and is not sequential Bayes. RL receives objective full-information source feedback. The acquired bounded score is not literal log-likelihood evidence; “high-depth” is not a psychological diagnosis. These hypothetical results do not establish a history of human childhood, obedience, norms, morality, altruism, or genetic separability from cognition.
