Interpretation and hard limits
The heatmap and trajectories are stored simulation outputs. Controls select a precomputed context or cell; the browser does not solve equations. rT and last-100-time-unit growth are finite-window population summaries. Invasion is conditional rare-mutant growth in the declared common retained resident culture and resource environment; it is not fixation probability. Survival conditions on final evidence and advice held fixed; it is not realized cohort survival through a changing environment.
For non-reset architectures, d=0 learners retain private knowledge into adulthood; they are not forced to relearn. Frozen adult conviction is an imposed no-adult-query policy, not an evolved dogmatism genotype. Confidence-based audit is also imposed and state-dependent. The oracle has privileged current reliability information and a finite-memory proxy; it is not a full Bayesian learner. The load scenario divides information exposure and does not simulate combinatorial reasoning. Source misinformation, actual signed-signal corruption, and true environmental reversal are distinct interventions. These hypothetical results do not establish human genetic separability, historical adaptation, norms, morality, or cooperation.
