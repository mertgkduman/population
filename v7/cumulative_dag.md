# Eight-node cumulative procedure DAG validation

This is an additional R4 validation fixture, isolated from V7’s authority and deference models. It does not alter a primary deference effect, and a positive result is not evidence for childhood deference, an authority adaptation, or novelty.

The fixture uses eight procedures with a real prerequisite DAG. The canonical graph has two parallel branches, `m0 → m2 → m4` and `m1 → m3 → m5`, merging at `m6 → m7`. The causal-scramble control instead has three roots and materially different parent relationships; it is not a cosmetic relabeling.

All people are blank at founding and birth. They inherit only a biological-parent identifier, never receipts, procedure state, or private discovery progress. A private candidate requires eight paid work tokens and succeeds only when an `.8` private physical candidate is correct and the actual prerequisites are possessed. Agents receive physical success/failure feedback; they do not read a hidden correct parameter or prerequisite map.

The accepted finite-resource foundation is retained exactly: resource cap/initial/regeneration `800/600/.06`, conversion `.75`, initial energy `8`, four tokens/person/season, maintenance `.5`, maturity/lifespan `3/18`, birth/newborn/residual-sink `6/4/2`, birth probability `.12`, prospective operational cap `256`, and horizon `60`. Safe harvesting withdraws two resources before a full procedure; only a functional eight-module program can make a four-resource full harvest. Every harvest has a central time token; all energy gain is an actual physical withdrawal. Private work, teacher work, pupil work, maintenance, deaths, and births have explicit energy bookkeeping. Same-season births never act.

## Declared solo bound

Eight private discoveries require at least 64 tokens. From a maximum 72-token lifetime, at most eight safe-harvest tokens remain. Initial energy plus those harvests is `8 + 1.5×8 = 20`, below maintenance plus private-learning debits `9 + 12.8 = 21.8`. This is a bound only for this declared physical acquisition protocol; it is not a universal cognitive or biological impossibility theorem. Offspring begin at energy four, making the individual bound tighter.

## Arms and outputs

The preregistered arms are transmission+innovation+retention, private-only, transmission-only without bootstrap innovation, no-retention (learned receipts and discovery progress cleared each season under the same private hardware), causal prerequisite scramble, and separately instantiated matched-total-work independent solvers. The paired solvers receive the same ex-ante feasible offer each season and never pool people, resources, or culture. All extinct, capped, paired-stop, and work-infeasible statuses remain exported.

Every acquisition creates a unique receipt/procedure ID and records owner/learner, module parameter and true prerequisites, parent procedure IDs, innovation root, immediate teacher/donor receipt, biological parent, time/energy debits, fidelity (fixed 1.0 before outcomes), and physical feedback. Representative complete DAGs for seeds 82901 and 82902 are exported, while every run records exact endpoints, season rows, event hashes, status, and streaming counts.

For every baseline seed, a held-out probe either retains `no_baseline_full_program` or takes a real acquired full program from a baseline owner. It transfers every module across paid source→B→C edges to blank B and C, records each edge and debit, then tests an unseen valid ordering of the two parallel branches plus prerequisite-violation and parameter-error physical failures. No expert is seeded to make this pass.
