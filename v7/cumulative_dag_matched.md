# Fresh matched-total-work independent-solvers control

This separately registered R4 control leaves the frozen cumulative-DAG fixture unchanged. It uses fresh pair seeds `82925..82948`, fixed horizon 60, and the same closed-resource settings. Every seed creates two physically separate populations: a transmission+innovation+retention population and a private-only independent solver. They never share people, resources, receipts, events, or cultural state.

At the start of every paired season, both populations receive the same ex-ante feasible total offer, `min(N_shared, N_independent) × 4`. Central allocation keeps every person at or below four work tokens. The raw output records every season's offered and realized work for both sides; any unequal or unrealized offer is an explicit infeasible/noncomplete status. Births are not deleted, pooled, or otherwise manipulated to force equality. All complete, censored, extinct, paired-stop, and infeasible pairs remain in the fixed denominator.

This is a validation comparator for paid reusable procedures only. It does not test or support a claim about authority, deference, childhood, novelty, adaptation, or the primary V7 effects.
