Model definitions and boundaries


The primary model has actual juvenile followers F and reproductive adults A. Age advances irreversibly; maturity is the fixed age 5, not an exponential waiting time. Offspring inherit the strategy pair and start with blank acquired B/K/T/H. Private cue accuracy is .8 at every age and deference level. Teachers offer acquired procedures; competent juveniles may also teach. Advice quality is endogenous, and age alone does not establish expertise.

N=F+A,Nt+1−Nt=Bt−Dt

B counts births, D deaths, and M surviving maturations per season. M transfers people between stages without changing N. Acquired factual belief Bᵢ, procedure memory Kᵢ, source calibration Tᵢ and action value Hᵢ are distinct from demographic symbols.

P(extra override)=ddomain·1age < 5·1advice conflicts with Q₀

Q₀ already uses private and social information; d=0 remains fully social. Main Q₀ is a greedy proxy. The exact two-episode solver is a separate tiny benchmark. The main mean-N landscape averages the full 240 seasons, including the common 60-season burn-in. It is not an invasion map or an equilibrium estimate.


Additional resource-control model
Ten blank founders; four work tokens per person/season; maturity 3; lifespan 18; horizon 60. Private discovery costs eight tokens per module, teaching debits teacher and pupil, and all gains require a physical harvest. Resource capacity is 800, renewal .06 and conversion .75. Complete functional recipes request 4 resource units, safe harvest 2. A birth debits 6 and credits the blank newborn 4; all residuals are sinks.

Rt+1=Rt+γ(Rcap−Rt)−Ut,Ei←Ei+ηui−costs

Resource plus living energy equals initial stock plus regeneration minus recorded sinks. The matched-work protocol offers the same feasible total effort to two independent populations. No people, knowledge or resources cross the pair. The carrier-restart assay separately removes all people and books an external input of ten naive replacements and 80 energy units; it is an experimental memory test. These models show ordinary knowledge reuse, without establishing knowledge beyond feasible solo acquisition.


Additional belief and action-value control
Each seed has a fixed training proposition and 40 actual childhood episodes, then cloned adults receive 20 updates. Private cues have .8 accuracy conditional on truth. The source conviction has .7 reliability and is counted once. Baseline/audience/free-audit arms use .8 audits; the explicitly reliable audit uses .95. The learner assumes switch hazard h=0 or .01; the experiment is stable or reverses truth. No adult inherited override or d-dependent rigidity is imposed.

Learning rate α=.15 at both ages. Approval is psychological reinforcement, never a material-credit bonus. Practice changes K only after actual paid success; K stays fixed during adult evidence updates. Strong static-world priors can delay reversal without irrational dogmatism. Curves use 24 independent seed blocks; repeated episodes and cloned arms do not increase that denominator. Some endpoint Wilson precision targets remain unresolved.


Planner and evolutionary interpretation
The bounded planner ranks query and audit by expected post-signal Q₀ minus actual fees and incremental operations. Twelve declared abstract deliberation units are paid on every option. Query accuracy .8 and audit accuracy .9 match execution; one information token cannot prevent the physical action. No global-optimal RL claim follows.

Finite lineage contrasts compare mutant and neutral-tag living descendants at T=80 in a shared resident environment. Positive finite differences do not establish asymptotic invasion, fixation, an ESS or stable polymorphism. Maturation age is fixed here, so no optimum childhood duration is identified. Authority-specific policies, independent-information provenance and stronger cumulative-culture tests remain incomplete. H4 obligation is not modeled. Technical verification cannot establish a historical adaptation.
