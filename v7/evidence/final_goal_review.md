## Scientific assessment

The strongest supported result is ordinary, costly cultural accumulation under the declared finite physical protocol—not an evolved advantage of additional authority deference. The R4 DAG demonstrates multi-generation reusable-procedure completion that an isolated learner cannot complete under that specific energy/work schedule, including a fresh equal-work comparison. Its causal-prerequisite scramble has the same endpoint outcome, so modification/prerequisite sensitivity is not established.

The corrected held-out deference result remains regime-dependent: vector `(.5,0)` mean-population contrasts span approximately `-0.390` to `+0.448`. This does not identify a unique optimum or specialized adaptation. The execution-priced planner is a bounded one-decision comparator and performs worse than ordinary in its three sentinel regimes; that is a valid negative result, not proof about global RL.

Adult information-equalization leaves factual posteriors unchanged across matched juvenile `d` histories while learned action values can differ. Static-prior reversal versus switching-prior reversal shows that persistence can arise from a world model and prior inertia. Irrational dogmatism and normative obligation were not generated or identified.

Finite paid-newborn lineage comparisons are precise for their registered `T=80` endpoint. They are not rare-mutant exponents, direct competition, fixation probabilities, stable polymorphism, or ESS evidence. Maturation age `tau=5` is fixed, not optimized.

## Remaining concrete scientific gaps

These are **unrun or partial designs**, not null findings. They do not block this bounded release because the corresponding stronger claims are explicitly withheld.

1. **`RISK-01` was not run.** No low-density random-projection/branching-growth validation exists. Minimal closure: preregister a small fixed seed block and compare the correct log/branching growth object against the analytic risk diagnostic. Until then, make no multiplicative-risk claim.
2. **No 120-season direct competition or rare-mutant growth assay ran.** Minimal closure: preregister one to three regimes and reciprocal/endpoint/nearby pairs, retain all ancestry/extinction outcomes, and report competition trajectories without adapting the seed schedule. Until then, make no invasion, fixation, polymorphism, or ESS claim.
3. **Cumulative modification dependence remains partial.** The R4 DAG lacks a nondegenerate modification-disabled arm with transmissible starting culture, and the changed prerequisite graph has identical endpoints. Minimal closure: acquire/bootstrap the same initial culture under paid rules, then freeze further modification in one arm and add a policy whose physical success depends on the changed prerequisites.
4. **Ecological provenance/authority attribution remains partial.** R4 proves the cognitive information and label mechanisms, but not their demographic effect in shared ecology. Minimal closure: embed the accepted independent/copied-root by visible/hidden-provenance and randomized-label interventions into paired corrected worlds with seed-block uncertainty.
5. **The strong learner comparison is bounded.** Candidate B and FeatureTrust are not global action-conditional learners; the accepted planner is one-decision and common-Q0. Minimal closure, only if a general-learning/adaptation claim is sought: preregister an action-conditional, provenance-aware longer-horizon learner with matched training, observations, operations, and executable opportunity costs.
6. **Normative obligation remains unmodeled.** Audience removal and learned habit are present, but countermand plus novel-context obligation discrimination is absent. This was optional in the controlling design and is not a release blocker; it must remain absent from claims.
7. **Primary maturation and teaching rules remain fixed/limited.** `tau=5` was not optimized, teacher content is lexical-first, and full source-selection robustness is incomplete. These are model-scope limits, not failed results.
