# V7 executive research summary

**Scientific verdict: PARTIAL. All numbers below are simulation outputs, not empirical estimates or evidence of historical adaptation.** V7 runs a variable-population, finite-resource model in which individuals acquire procedures, teach them, reproduce, and die. Newborns inherit strategy only; acquired belief, procedural knowledge, source estimates and action values start blank. Private evidence accuracy remains .8 across strategies. Primary maturation age is fixed at five seasons; this version does not estimate an optimal childhood duration.

The literature already establishes advantages and failures of social information, cultural accumulation, selective trust and informational cascades. V7 asks the narrower question: after a capable ordinary social learner has used both information sources, does an additional inherited tendency to override its judgment provide an advantage? Thus d=0 is already social; it is not an organism condemned to relearn everything independently. See `literature_map.md` for verified primary sources and competing precedents.

## What ran and what happened

- Corrected primary experiments include 504 discovery populations, 1,368 holdout populations, 288 calibration controls, 2,112 finite-lineage arms and 216 shock/memory-erasure records. The held-out candidate (.5 procedure override, zero local override) changes mean population by −.390 to +.448 across nine regimes. The signs vary. Local override often harms abundance in the exploratory screen. Neither a universal benefit nor equivalence has been established.
- Ordinary cultural knowledge does help in a separate strictly accounted experiment: at exactly equal total work, transmission raises mean population by 4.318 individuals (paired seed MCSE .607; 24 pairs). This tests reusable information, not extra authority sensitivity.
- A more demanding eight-module branching task produces 22.833 complete-program holders (MCSE .567) with innovation, transmission and retention; private-only, no-retention and transmission without bootstrap innovation produce none. In 24 fresh equal-work pairs, the corresponding endpoint is 2.083 versus zero (paired MCSE .574). A held-out paid source→B→C physical test passes in 23/24 blocks; the failed transfer is retained. The individual-acquisition impossibility bound applies only to the declared physical acquisition protocol. The changed-prerequisite graph produces identical outcomes, and a full modification-disabled control remains unresolved.
- Adult belief persistence can follow from a strong prior and an assumed static world. In a fixed-proposition assay, changing juvenile override while equalizing information leaves factual posteriors identical; learned action habits can differ. Allowing a small presumed switching hazard restores adaptation after reversal. This does not establish irrational dogmatism, obligation, or genetically encoded adult rigidity.
- Fresh information-history controls distinguish genuinely independent testimony from repeated copies. Exact posterior probabilities differ as expected when roots differ. An uninformative authority label affects an explicitly label-sensitive positive-control policy. That responsiveness is imposed for identification, not shown to have evolved.
- The resource-priced planner is a bounded one-decision comparator, not an optimal lifelong learner. Conditional descendant counts stop at T=80; they are not an asymptotic invasion exponent, fixation probability or ESS.

## What survived critical review

A real no-teacher hidden-information defect was found in the original frozen ecology. Those results are invalidated and preserved. A separately frozen correction was tested and rerun on fresh seeds. Reports and the offline dashboard route to the corrected data and separately labeled controls. Failed prototypes and source amendments remain inspectable.

The defensible contribution is a reproducible research instrument and a set of bounded mechanism contrasts. Ordinary cultural accumulation can work under noisy evidence and costly acquisition. This does not by itself identify an advantage of additional judgment deference, and no novel evolutionary mechanism is claimed. Further work must combine reliable source selection, stronger matched learning policies and shared cultural environments with actual long-run competition, while testing rather than assuming the mechanism that maintains adult beliefs.
