# R4 information, authority, provenance, and memory controls

`run_information_controls.py` executes two preregistered, fixed-proposition cognitive controls with fresh 24-seed blocks (`83351..83374` and `84351..84374`).  It freezes the source archive and registration before generating confirmation output; fixed preflight seeds `83350` and `84350` are not part of either confirmation block.

- AUTH-02 / PROV-01 (`83301..83324`): two initially blank tutors each pay for three .8-accurate private observations.  Their complete private histories, posterior convictions, paid costs, public messages, and physical roots are exported.  The independent arm transmits both genuinely acquired tutor histories; the copied arm repeats tutor A's conviction/root while preserving tutor B's independently acquired belief in the raw ledger.  Visible root-aware likelihoods are contrasted with a hidden-provenance `.5` mixture over copied and independent generators.  Uninformative peer/authority labels are assigned after evidence; the authority-sensitive response is an imposed policy positive control, not an adaptation result.
- AUTH-03 (`84301..84324`): a fixed-exposure 2x2 memory acceptance x juvenile override control uses 40 actual .8-private-signal training episodes and 20 adult reuse episodes.  Acceptance determines whether factual/procedural testimony and its root are stored; override only changes juvenile action after Q0.  Adult direct inherited override is zero.  Raw rows keep B, K, T, H, budget, immediate action, receipt, accepted memory, and later reuse separate.

The exact binary-history enumeration confirms `q_tutor = 3*.8^2 - 2*.8^3 = .896`, root-aware duplicate invariance for copied testimony, and the explicit hidden mixture.  With equal information and utility an extra override cannot beat the optimal equal-information baseline in expected payoff; that null/dominance statement is mathematical, not a novelty claim.  These reduced cognitive controls make no demographic, invasion, selection, historical-adaptation, or general authority claim.
