# V7 literature and novelty map

**Review date:** 2026-11-02  
**Scope:** targeted primary-source review for model design and defensible novelty; not a systematic review  
**Evidence register:** `v7/evidence/literature_sources.json`

## Bottom line

V7 should not claim novelty for “docility,” age-dependent openness, selective social learning, developmental social-learning programs, coevolving teaching schedules, procedural improvement without causal understanding, or culturally sustained inefficient practices. Each has direct precedent. Simon introduced docility as receptivity to social influence whose gross benefit can carry an altruistic cost; Acerbi, Enquist, and Ghirlanda modeled cultural transmission changing later openness; Deffner and McElreath coupled age structure, copying biases, demography, and adult updating; Maisonneuve, Lehmann, and Mullon coevolved learning schedules and teaching; and the Derex/Saral experimental line separates procedural improvement from causal understanding. [VERIFIED: L1, L2, L3, L4, L6, L11]

The defensible contribution is narrower and stronger: an auditable integration that **holds ordinary social learning and private ability constant while separately manipulating procedure-level conflict override, local-action override, epistemic authority credit, approval-seeking, and internalized normative value**. It then tests these mechanisms in matched causal assays and a resource-accounted age-structured ecology with source genealogy and invasion analysis. [ASSUMED: novelty is an inference from the targeted search, not proof of absence]

This distinction matters. A learner who uses testimony, demonstrations, consensus, or demonstrator performance is displaying ordinary social learning. A learner who follows a tutor against equally available contrary evidence displays an additional conflict response. A learner who behaves differently when observed may be complying for approval. A learner who privately judges a convention obligatory after the audience is removed may have acquired a normative commitment. A learner who maintains a factual proposition despite accessed, diagnostic counterevidence displays factual dogmatism. These are different outcomes and must never be collapsed into one scalar “deference” variable. [VERIFIED: L12, L18, L19, L20, L21-L24 establish relevant separations and alternatives]

## Literature map by design question

### 1. What is the null model of a competent learner?

The null is not a socially isolated individual. Morgan and colleagues show that people change their weighting of social and asocial evidence with demonstrator number, agreement, confidence, task difficulty, information costs, and relative performance. Rendell and colleagues’ tournament likewise establishes selective strategies in changing environments. Schultner and colleagues provide an especially demanding current comparator: feature-based reward learning can generate flexible social-learning strategies without a dedicated, fixed social bias. [VERIFIED: L5, L12, L13]

Therefore every V7 genotype should share the same sensors, message access, memory capacity, likelihood calculation, and opportunity budget. The no-extra-deference control must still observe, learn from, and evaluate others. Only the extra response to conflict—or separately the value assigned to authority, approval, or convention—may vary. Otherwise any advantage attributed to deference could simply be the benefit of receiving information.

A resource-rational planner is another appropriate comparator. Callaway and colleagues show how bounded planning operations can be selected according to their expected value and cost. V7 need not reproduce their task, but it should include a small-world policy that allocates finite verification or planning effort rather than granting “independent judgment” for free. [VERIFIED: L8]

### 2. What counts as accumulated technology?

Mesoudi and Thornton distinguish cumulative cultural evolution from mere transmission. Increasing the number of stored statements, copying confidence, or population agreement is not enough. V7’s procedure domain should produce measurable task-level performance through reusable, ordered components or prerequisites. [VERIFIED: L9]

The wheel-task lineage supplies an empirical anchor. Derex and colleagues found technological improvement without parallel growth in causal understanding. Saral, Ibáñez de Aldecoa, and Derex later showed that causal reasoning speeds early improvement while transmission can continue to raise performance without a causal structure; the reasoning advantage diminishes later. These results justify recording procedural performance and causal model accuracy separately. They do not justify setting reasoning to zero or treating all cultural performance as opaque copying. [VERIFIED: L3, L4]

Maisonneuve and colleagues make teaching and learning schedules endogenous in a theoretical cumulative-culture system, so V7 must compare against schedule evolution rather than claim it. El Aissati and colleagues provide a complementary field connection: group review in farming communities across Morocco, Mali, and Ghana improved final causal answers, indicating that local expertise can be distributed and selectively elicited. The mature model should allow acquired competence, not age or authority alone, to determine informational quality. [VERIFIED: L6, L16]

### 3. Why might ineffective practices persist?

Persistence has several ordinary explanations. Socially transmitted theories can narrow subsequent exploration whether they are useful or misleading. Cognitive appeal can preserve costly ineffective solutions by steering exploration. Highly refined products can favor simple copying or hyper-credulity because reconstructing the causal pathway is difficult. Observational learning can also reshape values, making a behavior attractive without any factual misinference. [VERIFIED: L14, L15, L17, L18]

These are rival mechanisms, not supporting labels for authority. V7 needs matched interventions: equalize content appeal; expose equally diagnostic evidence; make exploration cheap; preserve ordinary copying; remove audiences; and independently switch learned reward or normative value. If an inefficient procedure persists only because learners never encounter a revealing alternative, the result is canalized exploration, not dogmatism. If public behavior persists but private belief changes when the audience disappears, it is approval-sensitive compliance. If a convention remains privately obligatory while factual predictions update normally, it is normative commitment. If a factual posterior resists accessed counterevidence, that is the narrower candidate for dogmatism.

### 4. How should age, demography, and teacher choice enter?

Deffner and McElreath directly model age-structured populations, copy-old/copy-young biases, adult updating, environmental change, and demographic filtering. Pérez Velilla and Smaldino model own versus social learning, tag informativeness, demographic composition, frequency-dependent invasion, and filtering costs, with age cohort offered as one application of tag similarity. Their cost specification is deliberately reduced-form and chosen to permit interior optima; V7 should not import such a cost merely to manufacture a compromise. [VERIFIED: L7, L11]

Age must therefore be a life-history state, not a proxy for competence. Tutors should acquire procedural and local competence through their histories, and learners should observe noisy cues of that competence. Adult persistence should emerge from accumulated evidence, habits, sunk verification, role incentives, or explicit normative values—not from an unexplained hard-coded adult learning rate. Demographic output must follow realized survival, energy, production, and teaching costs.

### 5. What exactly is learned from a source?

Advice, observed action, factual testimony, and moral testimony are not interchangeable. Hertz, Bell, and Raihani distinguish advice from observation and immediate from long-term effects. Li finds that children and adults evaluate first- and second-hand moral and factual information differently across two cultures. Whalen, Griffiths, and Buchsbaum show sensitivity to shared evidence and sequential dependencies, while Xie and Hayes find that many participants nevertheless fail to distinguish dependent from independent testimony. [VERIFIED: L21-L24]

V7 should store message type, informational parent, behavioral model, and cultural lineage. The learner can then discount repeated descendants of the same original demonstration without assuming perfect human provenance reasoning. Both a normative Bayesian lineage-aware comparator and an empirically plausible lineage-insensitive policy are needed. This also prevents “ten copied assertions” from masquerading as ten independent observations.

## Required comparators and empirical connections

The minimum scientifically strong comparison set is:

1. **Reliability learner:** ordinary social/asocial integration with no extra conflict override. This is the main control.
2. **Feature-reward learner:** trust weights learned from observable source features and outcomes, following the logic of Schultner et al. [VERIFIED: L5]
3. **Lineage-aware Bayesian learner:** exact small-world integration that discounts shared evidence, paired with a lineage-insensitive policy. [VERIFIED: L21, L22]
4. **Resource-rational metareasoner:** finite verification/planning allocated by expected value. [VERIFIED: L8]
5. **V6 bridge:** the old scalar `d`, included only to show what changes when memory acceptance and action substitution are unbundled.
6. **Two-override learner:** separate procedure conflict override and fast local-action override.
7. **Authority-placebo control:** authority cue changed while content, competence, lineage, and incentives are matched.
8. **Approval/norm controls:** public versus private response, audience removal, and independent intervention on learned normative value. [VERIFIED: L19, L20]
9. **Value-shaping alternative:** observation changes preferences rather than evidence weights. [VERIFIED: L18]

Empirical calibration should use several narrow links rather than claim a single dataset validates the whole ecology: wheel/transmission tasks for procedure versus causal understanding (L3, L4); advice versus observation and persistence (L23); public/private response for compliance (L19); correlated-source judgments (L21, L22); and distributed local technical knowledge (L16). The model should reproduce qualitative contrasts before extrapolating to population-level selection.

## New falsifiable hypothesis beyond H1-H6

### H7 — Provenance-compression hypothesis

**Claim.** An apparent authority/deference advantage will arise when repeated messages copied from one lineage are treated as independent evidence, and it will shrink or disappear when accurate provenance is available or sources are truly independent.

**Test.** Use a preregistered 2×2 assay: independent versus copied message lineages crossed with visible versus hidden provenance. Hold message count, content, tutor competence, private evidence, and payoffs constant. Cross this with an informative versus uninformative authority cue. Record factual posterior, procedure choice, fast local action, public response, private response, and normative value separately. Then embed the same policies in the ecological model and compare invasion outcomes.

**Predictions.** Hidden copied lineages should inflate confidence and apparent conflict-following relative to visible copied lineages. Visible provenance should have little effect when sources are genuinely independent. A purely authority-driven policy should retain an authority-label effect after lineage is matched; a competence learner should follow actual reliability; an approval learner should change public behavior without the same private posterior shift. [ASSUMED: new hypothesis derived from L21-L22 and V7 construct separation]

**Rivals.** (1) Genuine epistemic authority priors: label effects persist after dependency correction. (2) Teacher competence: effects follow reliability, not label or genealogy. (3) Coordination or approval: behavior persists only under observation. (4) Redundancy-induced confidence: independent-source multiplicity matters even without authority. Whalen et al. make provenance-aware discounting plausible, whereas Xie and Hayes make systematic under-discounting empirically plausible; the direction is therefore genuinely open. [VERIFIED: L21, L22]

## Safe novelty language

The strongest defensible statement before running V7 is: **“Prior work establishes each of several component mechanisms, but we did not locate a model that holds ordinary social learning and private ability fixed while separately identifying procedure conflict override, local-action override, epistemic authority, approval sensitivity, and normative commitment within a source-genealogical, resource-accounted age-structured ecology.”** [ASSUMED]

Do not claim that V7 discovers docility, developmental conservatism, selective copying, teaching schedules, cultural opacity, inefficient-tradition persistence, public/private conformity, or correlated-testimony discounting. Do not claim that a bundled parameter estimates authority, that persistence without counterevidence is dogmatism, or that private agreement is necessarily normative commitment. Novelty should ultimately be attached to a demonstrated causal identification result or emergent ecological regime—not to the size of the simulator.
