# R2 recovery: causal ecology and sequential adult diagnostics

This extension is registered separately from V7's immutable frozen and corrected primary sources. It uses actual completed `CorrectedEcologyRun` worlds in sentinel regimes 1, 5, and 9, but its post-run observation interventions are bounded causal diagnostics—not selection, invasion, or demographic-evolution evidence.

For authority, identical observed message/evidence states receive a visible randomized label. The `d=(0,0)` label-neutral policy therefore has an algebraic null label effect: labels are absent from its input and cannot change its action. A registered `d=(.5,.5)` label-sensitive treatment instead tests the existing conflict-override operation; it has no direct material obedience reward. ITT records retain all message opportunities; conflict-conditional counts are secondary and name their denominators.

For provenance, copied messages expose the same actual physical root twice; independent messages expose two distinct actual root identifiers. Content is unchanged. Visibility determines whether root IDs are available to the agent; hidden roots remain evaluator-only, and unknown provenance is never interpreted as independent evidence.

The adult component is a distinct cognitive assay. Every seed receives twenty sequential free-audit, matched-likelihood updates in each evidence direction. It exports factual belief `B`, acquired procedure `K`, learned action value `H`, and source trust `T` separately. Direct inherited adult override is exactly zero and no rigidity coefficient is defined from `d`. Adequate symmetric factual revision means the result must not be called dogmatism; action persistence must not be called normative commitment.

Wilson intervals, where reported, use 24 seed-level ecological clusters only. Repeated records within a seed are correlated observations and are never pooled as independent binomial trials. Fixed caps retain unresolved precision.

## Registered measured result

The fixed confirmation ran 72 completed corrected-world jobs (24 fresh seeds × sentinel R1/R5/R9) and exported 13,824 ecological rows. These are replayed decision counterfactuals on completed, actual corrected-world acquired states and messages; they are not altered-population trajectories, demographic ITT estimates, selection evidence, or proof of naturally acquired authority sensitivity.

Across 1,728 matched `d=(0,0)` label-neutral pairs, the visible authority-label contrast changed zero actions. This is the algebraic null predicted by the policy definition, not evidence against all social learning. The optional `d=(.5,.5)` operational contrast also changed zero actions in the sampled terminal records because the observations were adult (`age >= tau`) and direct adult override is disabled; it therefore cannot be promoted to an authority-responsiveness result.

There were 3,456 independent-root provenance rows; all used distinct actual physical root IDs, preserved transmitted message content, and retained the rule that hidden/unknown provenance is not independence. The adult cognitive assay exported 960 rows: 24 histories × supporting/contrary evidence directions × 20 sequential matched-strength (`.8`) updates. All direct inherited adult overrides were zero and factual belief revised symmetrically, so factual dogmatism is unsupported. The assay may establish only learned-action-state trajectories, not normative commitment.

The ecological opportunity export lists 384 conflict, 13,824 purchase, 960 no-teacher, and 1,344 revision indicators over 13,824 records. Those are event-record accounting counts, not binomial sample sizes. At the fixed seed cap, 19/24 seed clusters contained a conflict; the Wilson half-width is `.156`, retained as unresolved rather than tightened by pooling correlated records.

## R2v2 juvenile identification and separate adult arms

The terminal-adult authority records above are retained as adult negative controls. They cannot identify juvenile direct override because the corrected model disables it after `tau=5`. R2v2 separately froze and ran 24 fresh seeds (`83101..83124`) in each sentinel regime. A passive policy wrapper captured only live corrected-world, teacher-present observations with actual age `<5`; paired unobserved replays had identical digests, so logging did not change the model's behavior or random stream.

The confirmation exported 210,876 juvenile decision rows, comprising 105,438 matched `d=(0,0)` versus the applicable `(.5,0)` procedure or `(0,.5)` local decision pairs. It observed 33,133 genuine conflicts and 16,567 counterfactual action changes; conflicts were not manufactured. Those many decision records are correlated within 72 live worlds, so they do not license an independent-binomial confidence interval, demographic ITT effect, selection claim, or estimate of naturally learned authority responsiveness. They identify the existing juvenile conflict-override operation under matched actual state/message records.

R2v2 also replaces the prior combined adult sequence with 192 cloned-history sequences: 24 seeds × four separately represented arms (`baseline`, `audience_removal`, `free_audit`, `evidence`) × two evidence directions. Each sequence has exactly 20 matched-likelihood updates, giving 3,840 exported rows with separate B/K/H/T states and zero inherited adult override. This remains a cognitive assay: factual dogmatism and normative commitment are unsupported.
